# Adverlyx Intelligence - Admin SOPs
## Standard Operating Procedures for AI Decision Support

---

## 1. WHEN TO TRUST AI RECOMMENDATIONS

### ✅ TRUST AI When:
- **Confidence level is HIGH** → AI has sufficient data
- **Recommendations align with platform norms** → Growth speeds, targeting are within typical ranges
- **User profile is complete** → Niche, engagement rate, follower count available
- **Risk assessment is LOW-MODERATE** → No red flags detected

### ⚠️ VERIFY AI When:
- **Confidence level is MEDIUM** → Partial data, AI may be extrapolating
- **Unusual recommendations** → Very aggressive targets, unfamiliar niches
- **New account types** → Agency accounts, celebrity accounts, brand accounts
- **Cross-border targeting** → Different platform behaviors in different regions

### ❌ OVERRIDE AI When:
- **Confidence level is LOW** → AI is guessing
- **Risk assessment is HIGH/CRITICAL** → Manual review required
- **User complaints contradict AI** → Real-world feedback trumps AI prediction
- **Legal/compliance concerns** → AI doesn't understand local regulations

---

## 2. WHEN TO OVERRIDE AI DECISIONS

### Mandatory Override Scenarios:
1. **Account flagged by Instagram** → Pause growth immediately, ignore AI speed recommendation
2. **User requests slower growth** → User preference always wins
3. **Engagement rate dropping rapidly** → AI may not catch this in real-time
4. **Multiple user complaints about same issue** → Pattern override needed
5. **Legal/regulatory request** → Immediate compliance required

### Override Process:
1. Document reason for override in admin notes
2. Update user's growth plan manually
3. Log override in AI activity log
4. Review AI recommendation accuracy after 7 days
5. Report systematic issues to platform team

---

## 3. HOW TO RESPOND WHEN AI FLAGS RISK

### Risk Level: LOW
- **Action**: Continue normal operations
- **Monitoring**: Standard weekly review
- **User Communication**: None required

### Risk Level: MODERATE
- **Action**: Increase monitoring frequency to daily
- **Monitoring**: Check engagement rates, follower quality
- **User Communication**: Optional proactive check-in

### Risk Level: HIGH
- **Action**: Pause growth, notify user
- **Monitoring**: Immediate account review
- **User Communication**: Required - explain temporary pause
- **Escalation**: Flag to senior admin within 24 hours

### Risk Level: CRITICAL
- **Action**: Immediate growth stop, account protection mode
- **Monitoring**: Manual review within 1 hour
- **User Communication**: Urgent notification with explanation
- **Escalation**: Immediate escalation to platform lead
- **Documentation**: Full incident report required

---

## 4. HOW TO EXPLAIN AI DECISIONS TO CLIENTS

### Do Say:
- "Our AI analyzed your account and recommends [X] for optimal safety"
- "Based on patterns from similar accounts in your niche..."
- "This recommendation prioritizes long-term account health"
- "Results typically vary based on content quality and engagement"

### Don't Say:
- "The AI said..." (makes it sound like we don't understand)
- "The algorithm decided..." (removes human accountability)
- Specific numbers or guarantees
- Technical AI/ML terminology

### Client Objection Handling:

**"Why is my growth so slow?"**
> "Your growth speed is optimized for account safety. Faster growth can trigger platform flags. We prioritize sustainable, long-term growth over quick spikes."

**"Can you make it faster?"**
> "We can adjust the approach, but faster growth increases risk. Let's review your account health first and discuss options that balance speed with safety."

**"The AI recommendation doesn't fit my situation"**
> "AI provides a starting point based on patterns. Your insight is valuable - let's adjust the plan together based on your specific goals."

---

## 5. AI ACCURACY REVIEW PROCESS

### Weekly Review (Admin):
- Compare AI growth plan predictions vs actual results
- Note patterns where AI over/under-estimated
- Flag any systematic issues

### Monthly Calibration (Platform Team):
- Review all AI override instances
- Analyze accuracy by niche, follower range, plan tier
- Adjust AI parameters if needed

### Quarterly AI Audit:
- Full accuracy report across all modules
- User satisfaction correlation with AI recommendations
- Identify training data gaps

---

## 6. ESCALATION MATRIX

| Issue Type | First Response | Escalate To | Timeframe |
|------------|----------------|-------------|-----------|
| AI gives wrong niche | Admin corrects | None | Immediate |
| AI risk assessment seems wrong | Senior Admin | Platform Lead | 24 hours |
| User reports AI recommendation caused issues | Platform Lead | Product Team | 4 hours |
| AI consistently wrong for a segment | Product Team | Engineering | 48 hours |
| Legal/compliance concern with AI | Compliance | Legal | Immediate |

---

## 7. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

### For Every AI Override:
- Date/time
- Original AI recommendation
- Override decision
- Reasoning
- Outcome (tracked at 7/14/30 days)

### For Every Risk Escalation:
- Risk level triggered
- Account details (anonymized)
- Actions taken
- Resolution status
- Lessons learned
